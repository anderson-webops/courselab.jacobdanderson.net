# ECE 6390 coverage

Reviewed November 19, 2026. The public collection contains 87 original lessons, 86 interactive models and 124 attempt-first questions, plus the Exam 1 and Exam 2 study narratives. Each lesson links to the narrative figure that explains it. Lessons are grouped by subject in course order; lecture notes, recordings and guided readings establish the material for each topic.

## Remaining depth

These subjects appear in the study narratives but do not yet have a separate lesson:

- Exam 2: FFT frequency-grid bookkeeping, reconstruction of samples by sinc interpolation, μ-law companding, differential and π/4 phase encoding, the sidebands created by carrier modulation, the GPS signal plan and navigation message, coding gain read at a fixed error rate, and G/T with an explicit link-margin readout.
- Exam 1: attitude control and pointing, solar-cell I–V curves, bent-pipe and regenerative transponders, power-source comparison, radiation resistance and images/folded dipoles, VSWR as a match measurement, rain-fade availability statistics, and orbit perturbations.
- The turbo-iteration lesson has a reference figure without an adjustable model.
- Several Exam 1 lessons carry one practice question; later lessons carry two.

## Compatibility and validation

Topic URLs, saved-question keys and control query parameters are unchanged; renamed lesson titles keep their routes. Validation covers numerical anchors recomputed from the narratives, every control at its limits, URL state, label bounds, light and dark themes at 320, 390 and 1440 pixels, local assets and CSP. Release evidence stays in the repository's ignored local work area.
